# Privacy

**What is collected:** a recruiter's name, work email, phone and LinkedIn URL, plus company —
only what's needed to invite their company to campus placements.

**Where it goes**
- The *PU Outreach Tracker* sheet (Automation tab). Keep sharing restricted to Placement Unit members.
- A Gmail draft in the capturing member's own BITS mailbox.
- Screenshots stay on your computer (optionally deleted after a successful draft:
  `delete_after_success` in config — it only ever deletes screenshots it recognised as LinkedIn profiles).
- `metrics.csv` on your computer holds **no personal data**: timestamps, outcome, company, row.
- OCR runs locally (Tesseract). Nothing is sent to any third-party service.

**Deletion on request:** delete the person's row in the sheet, the draft/sent mail in your
Gmail, and the screenshot if you kept it. Tell the team so nobody re-adds them.

**India's DPDP Act 2023 (general information, not legal advice):** it governs digital personal
data. In plain terms: use personal data only for a clear purpose, keep only what that purpose
needs, keep it secure, delete it when it's no longer needed or when the person asks, and be
transparent if asked. Rules and exemptions under the Act are still being notified and
interpreted — if the Placement Unit wants certainty, ask the institute's legal/compliance office.
